Distance from the initial belief

Wasserstein-2 distance W₂ is the minimum root-mean-square displacement of probability mass along θ. It has θ units and ranges from 0 to 1; zero means the original PMF. Lower means greater preservation.

W₂(p, q) = √[∫₀¹ (Q_p(u) − Q_q(u))² du], where Q is the inverse cumulative distribution (quantile function).

      Model conventions & interpretation

      A trajectory is one exploratory realization. Comparing strategies requires a specified loss and budget, repeated seeds, and evaluation on random inputs not used to choose the strategy. Preserving an initial belief and learning the true coin bias are different objectives.
